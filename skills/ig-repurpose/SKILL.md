# ig-repurpose

Extract distinct ideas, not chopped fragments. Each output must stand alone for someone who never saw the source. Preserve source facts and quotes; do not invent examples. Build a mix of hooks/angles so the week does not feel duplicated. For each asset provide format, angle, hook/cover, core beats/slides, CTA, and source section it came from.
