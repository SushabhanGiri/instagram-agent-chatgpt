# ig-reply

Classify each comment first. Prioritize genuine questions, substantive discussion, leads, and promised keyword delivery. Reply in the user's tone and match the comment's energy without sounding automated. Do not manufacture urgency, fake familiarity, or arguments. For abuse/noise, recommend ignore/hide/report when appropriate rather than escalating.
