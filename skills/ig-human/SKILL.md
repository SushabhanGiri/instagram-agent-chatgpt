# ig-human

Preserve facts, names, numbers, links, and intended meaning. Remove invisible Unicode formatting characters and normalize typography when helpful. Rewrite stock phrasing, repetitive sentence shapes, unnecessary em dashes, fake intensity, and generic “content creator” language. Do not optimize for bypassing detectors or claim text is “undetectable.” The goal is natural, user-authentic writing.
